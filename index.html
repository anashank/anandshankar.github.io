About Me

I'm a Senior Bioinformatics Scientist at Leidos supporting the National Cancer Institute (NCI), where I develop and maintain production-grade genomics pipelines that power large-scale cancer research.

With over 5 years of experience in NGS pipeline development, I specialize in variant calling, RNA-seq, and epigenomics workflows using tools like Nextflow, Snakemake, DRAGEN, and NVIDIA Parabricks. I've also benchmarked GPU-accelerated platforms to optimize cost and performance at scale.

My background spans bioinformatics, software engineering (including a full-stack internship at PayPal), and cloud infrastructure on AWS and Azure — letting me build tools that are as reliable as they are fast.
